About Paulina Bonilla

Hi there! My name is Paulina, and I’m a multimedia artist and graphic designer. I’ve created original vector artwork, graphics for print and digital media, brands and logos, and even this website itself! My skills and knowledge span traditional 2D art, graphic design, branding, web design, and motion graphics. I’m experienced with Adobe software and have a strong understanding of color theory and the elements and principles of design. I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, grow, and create. This portfolio showcases a little of what I do. Thanks for stopping by!
